How it works

  1. RDKit parses the SMILES string.
  2. Atoms become graph nodes and bonds become graph edges.
  3. A two-layer PyTorch Geometric GCN learns molecular representations.
  4. Global pooling creates one vector for the molecule.
  5. A small regression head predicts log aqueous solubility.
